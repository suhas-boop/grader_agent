Q1. Internship overview: role + hours + what you did (10 pts)

9–10: Clear org/team/context; hours stated; specific responsibilities + scope + concrete examples of work.

7–8: Hours stated; tasks described but missing some detail (scope/impact) or context.

4–6: Role/tasks vague and/or hours missing; limited clarity on what was done. (If hours are missing, max = 6)

0–3: Missing most elements; unclear what the internship was or what was done.

Q2. New things learned (10 pts)

9–10: 3+ specific learnings (tools, processes, domain, stakeholder skills) + explains how learned/applied.

7–8: Some specifics; limited explanation of application.

4–6: Mostly generic learnings; minimal examples.

0–3: Little to no learning articulated.

Q3. Surprises (8 pts)

7–8: 1–3 genuine surprises; explains why surprising + what changed in understanding/behavior.

5–6: Mentions surprises with some reflection, but shallow.

2–4: Surface-level or list-only; little insight.

0–1: Missing.

Q4. Interview positioning: how learnings help in interviews (12 pts)

10–12: 2+ interview-ready stories using STAR (Situation/Task/Action/Result); highlights strengths + outcomes/metrics; ties to target roles.

8–9: Clear connection to interview themes; at least 1 strong example; limited results/metrics.

4–7: Generic claims with weak/no concrete stories. (If no STAR/examples, max = 7)

0–3: Missing or irrelevant.

Q5. EMPAC insights/observations (10 pts)

9–10: Correctly uses EMPAC; 2+ internship-linked observations with examples showing EMPAC in action.

7–8: Mentions EMPAC with 1–2 relevant examples but limited depth.

4–6: EMPAC referenced vaguely or partially incorrect. (If EMPAC has no internship-linked observation, max = 5)

0–3: Missing or incorrect with no evidence.
